# Evidence and pending decisions

This file is for setup provenance and pending personal decisions, not startup instructions. Unconfirmed personal values are not active preferences. Runtime problem workspaces and their linked occurrences, cause hypotheses, repair attempts, and checks belong in private `.maintenance/` records instead of accumulating here.

{{evidence_records}}

Each record should identify the requirement, method, quality criterion, or candidate; its narrow source pointer; the user's statement or a short summary; scope; confirmation or explicit delegation; and any superseded statement. Distinguish personal-value evidence from a method inferred to repair an established goal, and distinguish both from a hypothetical contrast. A current-task correction alone does not establish a universal preference. For multiple sources, retain their roles, reading boundaries, and sampling limits without treating the first or most frequent pattern as authoritative. Do not retain raw conversations, credentials, unrelated personal information, or project rules as personal defaults.
